# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and constructive community. All
participants are expected to:

- treat others with respect and professionalism;
- welcome different backgrounds, perspectives, and levels of experience;
- give and receive feedback constructively;
- focus criticism on ideas and behavior, not people; and
- protect confidential, personal, and security-sensitive information.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, personal
attacks, deliberate disruption, unwelcome sexual attention, and publishing
private information without consent.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to the project maintainers through the repository
host's private reporting channel. Include enough context to investigate, but do
not include secrets or unnecessary personal information.

Maintainers will review reports fairly, protect confidentiality where
practicable, and may remove content, restrict participation, or take other
actions appropriate to the situation.

This code of conduct applies in project spaces and whenever someone is
representing the project in public.
